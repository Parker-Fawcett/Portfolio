Bios

Short bio

Parker Fawcett is a high school student who works as an AI Skills Engineer at CHG Healthcare and is co-founder and CEO of Skora, a college-admissions SaaS platform. He created Rebuild Dossier, an open-source tool for specifying and testing AI-assisted app rebuilds, and has authored papers on both coding-agent verification and quantitative finance.

Longer bio

Parker Fawcett is a software builder and independent researcher based in Herriman, Utah, currently attending Herriman High School and the Jordan Applied Technology Center. At CHG Healthcare, he works in Enterprise Data & AI on AI-assisted automation and data workflows, a role he moved into after a summer internship. He is also co-founder and CEO of Skora, a college-admissions SaaS platform with paying counselor customers, and operates several other independent software products under his holding company, Fawcett Capital LLC.

His research spans two areas. Rebuild Dossier is an open-source MCP server that extracts an existing app’s interface contracts and produces a test-backed specification for a coding agent to use during a rebuild; his 2026 arXiv preprint, Rebuild Dossier: Mechanically-Enforced Specs for Agentic App Rebuilds, and What Model-Tier Failures Reveal, reports both successful and failed evaluations of that process. A separate 2026 paper on SSRN, When AUC Survives but Portfolios Do Not, audits predictive multiplicity in a machine-learning equity-selection model, showing that a portfolio's actual holdings can shift substantially while a standard accuracy metric barely moves.
