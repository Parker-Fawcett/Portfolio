Bios

Short bio

Parker Fawcett is an AI Skills Engineer at CHG Healthcare and an independent software researcher. He created Rebuild Dossier, an open-source tool for specifying and testing AI-assisted app rebuilds, and authored a 2026 arXiv paper evaluating the approach.

Longer bio

Parker Fawcett is a software builder and independent researcher based in Herriman, Utah. At CHG Healthcare, he works in Enterprise Data & AI on AI-assisted automation and data workflows. He created Rebuild Dossier, an open-source MCP server that extracts an existing app’s interface contracts and produces a test-backed specification for a coding agent to use during a rebuild. His 2026 arXiv preprint, Rebuild Dossier: Mechanically-Enforced Specs for Agentic App Rebuilds, and What Model-Tier Failures Reveal, reports both successful and failed evaluations of that process. He also builds independent software products and studies the reliability of AI development tools.
